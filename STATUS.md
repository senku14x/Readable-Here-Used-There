## H3 headline (2026-09-09, end of session; wording corrected after an external audit)

**The claim, stated at the strength the interventions support.** In these copying tasks, donor information installed in the **carrier** residuals changes downstream answer preferences much less than the same donor information installed at the **source** positions. Carrier transplants nevertheless produce content-specific answer shifts when the recipient lacks the source information. The pattern holds in a hybrid and in a dense Qwen model of the same depth and width. **These experiments establish differences between the effects of interventions at different positions; they do not identify the maintained representation or its natural computational role.**

Supporting results, each scoped to what was measured:
- **Position asymmetry.** Replacing source residuals reproduces the natural answer swing (share ≈ 1.00). Replacing only the carrier — which installs the donor value at the readout on J_NP, RESID_P and LOGITS — shifts the answer margin by 0.02–0.27 of that, depth-independently, with matched random writes inert. The carrier effect is **small but real and content-specific** (+1.75 nats sum report, +6.19 two-hop on the primary model); it is *not* zero and should not be called "ignored".
- **The source route survives clamping the carrier outputs.** Holding the carrier block outputs at the recipient's own values leaves 0.73–1.00 of the source-induced shift. The reported 0.000 is the *donor-related readout change* at those positions, not the absence of representation there.
- **Donor-value readouts appear at the question positions under that clamp** (78–93 % of their free level). That shows a route reaching the question without passing through the clamped carrier outputs. It does **not** distinguish fresh computation at the question from retrieval of an already-computed value at the source positions or transmission through other unpatched positions; "rebuilt at the question" is an interpretation, not an identified computation.
- **Carrier information is causally usable when the source is absent.** With the operands hidden or the clue made unanswerable, transplanting a real run's carrier states makes that run's value win the scored candidate set (28/32 sums; 28/40 and 32/40 two-hop), identity-specific in both directions, random inert. **Endpoint precision:** on the sum organism the greedy first token is a complete correct number word or numeral in 14/32, with 9 further cells emitting an incomplete leading digit whose completion these forwards did not measure; the dense model's candidate-set result is 13/32. The transplant moves whole carrier residuals, which carry more than the lens-readable value, so this does not isolate a precomputed sum.
- **Consumer-dependent clamp effect (control-repaired 2026-09-09).** Clamping the sum-word naming plane at the question tokens (emission position excluded) lowers the report margin by 38.8 % on the primary model (12.8 % dense) while a norm-matched orthogonal clamp lowers it 0.2 %; parity moves ≤ 0.03 nats under either. The report consumer draws about 40 % of its margin on that plane; parity's insensitivity to *that plane* is not evidence that parity never uses the sum. The clamp itself is partial (bf16 rounding, weakness 1 below).
- **The paper's own intervention, decomposed by position (`two_hop_bridgeswap.md`, 2026-09-09).** A lens-coordinate swap of the two-hop intermediate at all positions from the clue on moves the answer 0.41 of the full-residual ceiling on the sequence-log-prob endpoint (12/12 items, 16/48 flips; decoy swap inert; 0.38 on dense Qwen3-32B). Split by position it is roughly additive: question turn 0.20, clue span 0.09, copied carrier 0.07 (each 12/12 on margin, 0/48 flips), although the carrier swap installs the swapped intermediate at the carrier readout as strongly as the whole swap. The answer-word swap is larger at every position set (0.63 overall), consistent with Nanda's replication. This is the position asymmetry for the representation the paper intervenes on, not only for whole residuals; it does not separate computation-from-clue-residuals from re-reading the clue at the question.
- **Withdrawn, and kept withdrawn without upgrading:** the recompute-cost account of the arithmetic-vs-fact carrier gap. The ladder did **not** preserve competence or installed-readout strength, so it shows that manipulation failed to isolate recompute cost — not that recompute cost is irrelevant. The boundary account is separately disconfirmed by the dense model (two-hop boundary 0.97 there, gap persists). What remains is an unexplained difference between two task families that also differ in relations, prompts, answer distributions and readout strength; "factual versus computed latent" is a label for the gap, not an established cause.
- **Method note, narrowed.** A successful sum-word coordinate swap that moves the report consumer and not parity shows such a swap is **insufficient evidence of substituting a generally usable value**, because the swap directions name the emitted answer. It does not establish that coordinate swapping is generally answer steering; the paper's reasoning experiments intervene on intermediates distinct from the emitted answer, which this project has not reproduced.

**Known control weaknesses (do not cite the affected rows without them).** Weaknesses 1 and 2 recorded after the 2026-09-09 audit (orthogonal clamp not norm-matched, `clampsplit` without a control, requested-norm logged as realized) are **closed by `query_local_workspace_clampfix.md`**: with a norm-matched orthogonal clamp of identical per-position write size, report drops 38.8 % under the naming-plane clamp and 0.2 % under the control, parity moves 0.03 nats under either; the "≈ 40 % mediation, parity bypasses that plane" reading is licensed again. Two remain:
1. **Coordinate-level writes are rounding-limited in bf16.** The post-cast realized write for the question-token clamps is ρ ≈ 0.53–0.59, κ ≈ 0.42–0.48 (requested norms 0.2–0.5); answer-only clamps ρ 0.77–0.86, κ 0.69–0.78. The project gate (ρ ∈ [0.9, 1.1], κ ≥ 0.99) is **not met** by any `CoordClamp`/`CoordSwap` row, past or present; the matched controls carry the same rounding and are inert, so effects are specific, but every clamp is a partial clamp and every coordinate null is a null against a partial write. Repair (float32 hooked outputs) is registered, unrun.
2. Mask rows: masking the consumer→source read is **not** a complete cut even on a dense model, because the source → carrier → consumer path stays open; and the correct comparison is over the masked baseline, where the carrier effect rises from +0.66 to +1.14 nats (≈ 1.7×), not the ×13 implied by comparing against the unmasked baseline.
