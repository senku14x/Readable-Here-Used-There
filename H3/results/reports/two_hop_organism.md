**What this adds.** The pattern established on arithmetic holds for a factual bridge: the maintained copy is real, content-specific, secondary while the source is readable, reconstructed at the question, and consumed when the source is gone. The one quantitative difference is its share while the source is visible — 0.27 here against 0.08 for the sum, and 12/48 flips against 0/32. A cheap recomputation (adding two visible numbers) leaves the copy nearly unused; a lookup that must be re-resolved leaves it doing about a quarter of the work. **Correction (same day, `recompute_cost_ladder.md`): the recompute-cost reading of this gap was tested inside the arithmetic organism and not supported** — with the boundary held fixed at 0.98–0.99, raising the difficulty of the computation *lowers* the carrier share (t3 − t1 = −0.10, 0/8). What co-varies with the gap across organisms *on this model* is the boundary itself (block-36 source share ≈ 1.00 for arithmetic, 0.58 here). **That account is now disconfirmed** (`replication_qwen3_32b/RESULTS.md` §8.2): on Qwen3-32B the two-hop boundary is 0.97, i.e. as complete as arithmetic's, and the carrier shares are still 0.02 (arithmetic) versus 0.23 (facts) within that one model. So the gap survives controlling for the boundary, and the ladder already showed it is not produced by computational difficulty. The domain difference is real and its cause is open.

**Scope and what this is not.** Twelve items from four relation families (two of them a whole-clue paraphrase rather than an entity swap), four carriers, one model, thinking off. The source intervention replaces the entire clue span, so it changes entity, bridge and answer together: nothing here attributes the effect to the bridge entity specifically, which would need a direction-level intervention on the intermediate alone. **Relation to Nanda's review:** he reports that on this model the released multihop set is too easy, with answer swaps dominating, and advises recalibrating the paper's evals to model capability. Our competence of 0.96 is consistent with "easy"; our organism differs in putting the clue in turn 1 behind a copy task with a question that never restates it, so the answer cannot be read locally. This is a difference in design, not a corroboration of his result, and it is reported as such.
