## 5. Interpretation and next decision

**Observation.** S (pointed - unpointed word transfer) is CI-clear and 8/8 in **all four** presentation x wording renderings, at both windows, on J_NP / RESID_P / LOGITS (Holm p <= 1e-6; J_NP L51-59: quoted_copy +4.10, quoted_directed +6.66, bare_copy +4.19, bare_directed +6.24). Damage nil (top-1 retention 1.000). quoted_copy reproduces the tagged_selection evaluate S bit-identically (ratio 1.0), confirming the model is deterministic on identical inputs and this cell is a faithful re-run of the established organism.

**What this licenses.** Selection -- the tagged instruction routing the pointed source's transfer above the unpointed -- is a property of relevance instructions on this model and readout, **not** specific to the quoted-and-introduced copy scaffold. It survives (i) bare, unquoted presentation and (ii) the paper-style directed wording ("Hold X in mind as you write"). This is the strongest available H1 scope result for selection: the effect is not an artifact of the one prompt family used to establish it.

**Two secondary findings, stated precisely.**
- **Presentation (quoted vs bare) barely affects S** (main effect on S: -0.17 [-0.31, -0.03] at L51-59, 2/8; -0.06 at L48-50): bare unquoted words are treated as selectable sources; quoting is not required.
- **Directed wording produces LARGER selection** than copy wording (main effect on S: +2.30 [+2.12, +2.49] at L51-59, 8/8; pointed transfer +8.58 vs +5.41). But the *character* differs: under copy wording the unpointed sources are suppressed **below** control (Q<0, amplify-and-suppress); under directed wording they are not (Q>0) -- the directed wording amplifies the pointed source strongly while the unpointed sit slightly above the weaker "came up in conversation" control. Read the wording contrast on **S** (which excludes the control arm), not on Q/U: the Q sign flip is partly the different registered control tails ("occurs one time" vs "came up in conversation").

**Caveats.** Evaluation scale but on one tag head (all four renderings keep the "Here are three words (A)(B)(C)" head, so the tag mechanism exists throughout -- this isolates presentation and wording, it does not test selection without a tag head). The pure direct organism (no copy bridge) and the single-word 2x2 are the `single` stage (not run here); the earlier direct-organism pilot null is still open there. S compares length-matched arms within a rendering; cross-rendering S comparisons mix selection strength with the overall transfer scale under each wording. Different instruments on the same forwards are robustness, not independent samples.

**Next decision.** All four renderings have S CI-clear, so per design section 6 the `directions` stage (native vs transferred fits) is warranted and the `single` stage (single-word 2x2 + the pure direct organism, which would resolve the open pilot null) is the registered next run within this experiment. Alternatively, selection generality is now established, so `selection_localization` (H1.10) -- the mechanistic "where is the selection state" question (project_status Q1) -- is the higher-value branch.
