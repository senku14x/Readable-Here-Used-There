- Same-source (slot 0) bitwise equal to clean and layers < 36 identical between futures asserted per cell at generation; donor geometry asserted per swap. Damage above.

## 5. Interpretation and next decision

**Observation (this setup).** Pointing at one of three tagged category labels and replacing its source span from block 36 with the partner-triple category selectively raises the pointed category's four *unshown* members' readability: S_member = +0.82 [+0.76, +0.88] at L51-59 (8/8 triples, 6/6 categories; +0.17 [+0.15, +0.19] at L48-50), with unpointed sources suppressed *below* control (Q = -0.08, 0/8) and instruction gain U = +0.73. The same selective profile holds on the label (S = +2.42 at L51-59), an independently-fitted RESID_P category-member axis (S = +1.33), and the model's own output logits (S = +0.83). The transfer is coherent across the whole member set -- 24/24 donor members up, 24/24 source members down -- with no carrier damage (delta-NLL <= 2e-4, top-1 retention 1.000). The member instrument is validated at L51-59 by the Stage-0 gate.

**What this licenses (calibrated).** Under these conditions the tagged instruction selects category-*associated* content, not merely the literal label token: the selectively-gated signal reaches member tokens that never appear anywhere in the prompt, coherently across all four members of each category, on two differently-constructed readouts and the output logits. This rules out the pure lexical-echo account (only the quoted label transfers). Scope: named quoted categories, this rendering, this readout, primarily L51-59, **extension scale** (6 shared identities, 8 triples, overlapping folds) -- a weaker evidential footing than the word-level tagged_selection result it extends.

**Strongest surviving alternative -- do not overread.** Member-token *readability* rising is not evidence the model *represents* the individual members. Installing category content ("tools") can raise every tool-member readout if member J-vectors (and plain-sentence member centroids) are correlated with the category-level content -- i.e. a single transferred "category-ness" direction, read out on correlated member tokens, reproduces this entire pattern. The RESID_P axis is fit independently of the J-lens but still cannot separate category-level from member-specific content (both project onto the member centroids). The design (section 10) makes no claim that a label "represents" its members; the endpoint is member readability. So: *category-associated content transfers selectively* is supported here; *member-specific representation* is not.

**Caveats.** (i) The member instrument is validated only at L51-59; at L48-50 the Stage-0 gate fails (CI includes 0) and tools/furniture members move the wrong way under maintenance -- the instrument is uneven across categories. S_member is nonetheless positive at both windows because the donor-minus-source swap contrast (decoys cancel) is more sensitive than the maintain-minus-absent presence gate, and L51-59 carries the bulk. (ii) The P0(pure) vs mixed confusion split is underpowered (n=2 pure triples); point estimates show no within-superclass confusion reducing S, but this is not a powered null. (iii) Different instruments on the same forwards are robustness, not independent samples.

**Next decision.** Decision tree (design section 11) outcome: gate PASS + S_member > 0 with Q <= 0 in both clusterings => selection of associated content licensed => **run Stage 2 (`directions`)**: does a fitted category "state" direction act as a selective gain (as d_shared does for words), and does a word-fit direction transfer to category sources and a category-fit direction to word sources (the design section 6 / H1.7 2x2)? Stage 2 is a GPU battery. The competing use of effort is `scaffold_generality` (H1.7), which the handoff ranks as the highest remaining information value. Researcher decision: run Stage 2 directions, or bank this result and move to scaffold_generality.
