## 5. Interpretation and next decision

**Observation (pilot, n=3 pairs, fitting carriers).** Moving the fitted block-35 coordinate ĝ to the recipient word's opposite-arm natural level, with the source counterfactual introduced only from block 36, changes the incremental transfer in the predicted reciprocal directions on every instrument: ΔC_M < 0 (0/3 positive) and ΔC_N > 0 (3/3) for J_NP at both windows, RESID_P at both windows, and the model's own logits. I/A is 0.5–0.7: the move reproduces roughly half of the natural maintain-versus-mention difference in transfer. Dose is monotone at ×0.5, ×1, ×2. Realized writes are exact (ρ 1.000–1.001, κ ≥ 0.9998); carrier damage is nil (ΔNLL ≤ 1e-4, top-1 retention 1.00). With n=3 the cluster-t intervals are wide; the L48–50 interval on J_NP excludes zero by 0.0006 and the Holm-adjusted p across the two windows is 0.078. This is a pilot reading, not an estimate.

**Specificity is not established, and the pattern is informative.** No panel direction exceeds |I_ĝ|, but the two with the largest cosine to ĝ reproduce most of it at the same signed α_t: at L48–50 `pc01` (cos 0.30) gives I = +0.122 vs ĝ's +0.126 and `cov01` (cos 0.18) gives +0.114; at L51–59 they give 0.52 and 0.53 vs 1.07; on LOGITS 1.06 and 1.04 vs 1.96. Both are reciprocal-signed 3/3. The four isotropic directions (cos ≤ 0.02) give small or opposite-signed effects; the two unrelated semantic axes (cos ≤ 0.013) give small positive effects at the late window only (0.30, 0.16 vs 1.07). Read together: the interaction scales with a direction's alignment to ĝ, and high-variance directions of the block-35 residual overlap ĝ enough to carry a large share of it. This is the same pattern the earlier pilot found before it ĝ-orthogonalized its panel (A1V2-L-4, A1V2-S). Under the project's H1.5 the conclusion at this stage is "generic state sensitivity cannot be excluded by an unprojected panel"; the ĝ-orthogonalized complement is the discriminating control and has not been run.

**The move also changes resident content.** With no source change, the ĝ move shifts the pair margin (M +0.09, N −0.10 at L48–50; +0.45, −0.65 at L51–59) and, in the mention arm, raises the introduced word's rank-1 surfacing over L24–59 from 0.00 to 0.50, matching the maintain arm's 0.50. The coordinate therefore acts on already-present content as well as on future transfer; the project's "content installation" alternative is not separated from "shared task modulation" by the pilot, since both effects are present with the same sign.

**Random-column floor.** The interaction on the named pair margin (+0.126 / +1.07) is far above the interaction on 64 random vocabulary columns (p95 0.005 / 0.008), so the effect is column-specific, not a global readout shift.

**Decision.** Before any evaluation-bank forward: (1) add a ĝ-orthogonalized panel (the same 12 directions with their ĝ component projected out, plus 4 covariance-matched directions sampled in the ĝ-orthogonal complement), run on the same pilot cells at the same signed α_t; (2) add a native-level comparison for `pc01` and `cov01` (moved to their own arm levels) to separate "carries ĝ" from "is an independent handle". Only if ĝ retains its interaction while the orthogonalized panel does not is the evaluation run on the 16 bank pairs worth its 4,000 forwards. The design amendment will be recorded in `H1/design_specs/task_state_modulation.md` before it runs.
