## 5. Interpretation and next decision

**Competition: under a pointer, selection does not dilute with the number of competitors (n = 4 sets).** The pointed focal source's transfer is flat from k = 2 to k = 6 on every instrument (J_NP L48–50: 0.50, 0.43, 0.38, 0.40; L51–59: 5.5, 5.1, 4.3, 5.4; RESID_P 0.18–0.19; a 1/k budget would give 0.17 at k = 6 from the k = 2 value), and selectivity S is CI-clear at every k (4/4 throughout). Without a pointer the focal word's transfer does fall with k (J_NP L48–50 0.21 → 0.05; L51–59 2.0 → 1.0; RESID_P 0.010 → 0.001): competitors dilute an unselected source, and the pointer overrides that dilution. The single-source prompt (k = 1, "Here is one word") gives a lower pointed transfer than k ≥ 2 on the lens (0.21 vs 0.38–0.50) though not on the logits (7.9 vs 6.8–10.8); it is a different prompt and is reported as the reference level, not interpreted.

**Cue: relevance is re-assigned mid-carrier at the late window and in the logits.** On the post-cue positions, switching the pointer from A to B lowers source A's transfer and raises source B's relative to the same-tag cue: J_NP L51–59 C_A −1.57 [−2.06, −1.08] (0/8), C_B +0.81 [+0.37, +1.24] (8/8), (C_B − C_A) +2.37 (8/8); LOGITS C_A −6.98 (0/8), C_B +2.26 (8/8); RESID_P L51–59 C_B +0.29 (7/8), C_A −0.35 (ns). At L48–50 the switch cue lowers both sources relative to the same-tag cue (J_NP C_B −0.24, 0/8; RESID_P −0.10, 0/8): at the early window the re-assignment is not yet visible and the cue reads as a general reduction. The registered contrast (switch − same-tag) therefore supports a textual re-assignment of relevance that is realized at layers ≥ 51 and in the output distribution.

**The same-tag cue is not neutral.** Relative to no cue, "Actually, keep the word tagged A in mind instead" lowers A's transfer at L51–59 (−2.07, 0/8) and RESID_P (−5.9, 0/8) and raises B's on every instrument (J_NP L48–50 +0.34, 8/8). A re-instruction sentence that says "instead" changes the profile even when it names the same tag. This is why the switch − same-tag contrast is the registered estimand and the none condition is a reference only.

**Both fitted coordinates move with the switch.** On the post-cue positions the ĝ coordinate rises by +0.99 [+0.85, +1.13] (8/8) and the d_shared coordinate by +1.82 [+1.65, +1.99] (8/8) under switch relative to same-tag, about 20 % of the pointed-minus-control gap along d_shared. The earlier pilot found its frozen coordinate did not move with a mid-carrier switch (CP-5); here, on evaluation materials and with a natively fit direction, it does. This is a readout of the coordinates, not an intervention.

**Licensed (H1.8 competition and re-assignment, evaluation materials, this rendering).** Selective source influence persists over 1–6 competitors (this is not a capacity or shared-budget result: one selected item with more distractors does not test simultaneous maintenance); an unselected source dilutes with competitors and the pointer removes the dilution; a mid-carrier textual re-assignment redirects transfer from the old to the new source at layers ≥ 51 and in the logits, and the pointed-minus-control coordinate moves with it. Not licensed: an internal pointer change independent of text; anything about the early window beyond "not yet visible"; anything behavioral.
