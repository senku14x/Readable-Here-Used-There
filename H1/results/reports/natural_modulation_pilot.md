Prominence agrees: the word reaches lens rank 1 at some interior position in layers 51–59 in 50 % of maintain cells and in ≤ 12 % of mention cells.

**The control matters more than the instruction at the default window.** maintain − plain mention is null at L48–50 on J_NP (+0.05, 3/8), J_CB (−0.00, 4/8), R_CB (−0.03, 4/8) and RESID_P (+0.03, 5/8), while mention − plain mention is negative (J_NP −0.28, 1/8; J_CB −0.19, 0/8; RESID_P −0.11, 2/8). The project's control depresses readability below a bare mention; at L48–50 the maintain instruction does not raise it above a bare mention. This replicates the earlier pilot's R1-5. At the late window the picture changes: maintain exceeds plain mention on RESID_P L51–59 (+1.32, 7/8), RESID_P L24–59 (+0.32, 7/8) and LOGITS (+1.91, 8/8), and J_NP levels at L51–59 are 2.35 (maintain) vs 1.15 (plain mention) vs 1.18 (mention). So the instruction's effect beyond mention is a late-band effect in this organism, not a default-window effect.

**Suppression tails.** "That word is irrelevant here" lowers readability below the mention control on J_NP (−0.24, 0/8) and RESID_P L36–50 (−0.05, 0/8). "Never think about that word" lowers mean presence (RESID_P L36–50 −0.18, 0/8; LOGITS −0.78, 0/8; J_NP ns) yet produces lens rank-1 surfacing at layers 53–59 in 7/8 words, at the same function-word positions (" the", " for") where maintain surfaces. The mean-presence and peak-rank metrics disagree under negation; both are reported, neither is privileged. This is an observation on 8 words and is not interpreted further here.

**Direct organism.** Under the copy-adapted rendering the released focus phrasing beats its mention phrasing by +1.43 (8/8) at L48–50 and +2.55 (8/8) at L51–59, with rank-1 surfacing 0.63 vs 0.00. Under the direct rendering (no copy instruction in the user turn) the mention phrasing's own presence is high (1.03 at L48–50) and the contrast is not CI-clear (+0.20, 5/8), while surfacing still separates (0.50 vs 0.00). The direct and copy-adapted organisms differ mainly in the mention baseline, as the earlier pilot's DM-5 also found.

**Instrument note (correction).** The float32 diagnostic z = n/r in this run used the final norm's stored weight as the gain; this architecture's RMSNorm applies 1 + weight (mean weight 0.96), so the diagnostic is scaled by roughly one half and is not used for any claim. The implementation-precision bf16-path score is the model's own computation and is the instrument. `common/scripts/readout.py` is corrected for subsequent runs.

**Decision.** Natural modulation relative to the project's control is established at pilot scale on this organism; relative to plain mention it exists only in the late band. Proceed to Package 2 with the readout window L48–50 as the registered primary and L51–59 as a registered secondary window (chosen on this calibration, before any Package 2 forward), and with plain mention retained as a co-primary control in every natural comparison. Package 1 decisions are recorded in `H1/design_specs/package1_decisions.md`.
